## About the Certification

**Exam Numbers:** 220-1101 (Core 1) and 220-1102 (Core 2)
The CompTIA A+ certification validates foundational IT skills and knowledge, covering hardware, software, networking, troubleshooting, and security fundamentals.

---

## Key Skills Covered

- Hardware and peripherals
- Operating systems (Windows, macOS, Linux)
- Networking and internet connectivity
- Mobile devices and cloud computing
- Virtualization and cloud services
- Security and compliance
- Troubleshooting and support
